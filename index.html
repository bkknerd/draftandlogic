    Iron Clad

    A live odds terminal that shows its working

    Iron Clad is an independent sports analytics site covering MLB, NFL, NBA, NHL and WNBA. It streams current market prices, ranks three daily parlays by probability and value rather than payout, and explains each pick in plain English.

    Standout featureShowing the working. Every parlay sets the model's probability against the market's price, with the edge and a 10,000-run simulation, so nothing is a black box.

    • Daily parlays with reasons and a 10,000-run simulation
    • Live board that refreshes itself
    • Every pick graded automatically, wins and losses
